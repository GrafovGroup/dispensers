**Domain:** dispensers.ca
**Repository:** GrafovGroup/dispensers
**Model:** Direct Canadian B2B commercial dispenser product and service provider
**Scope:** Phase 0 only — research, strategy, architecture, and implementation report. Do not build the website yet.

## Standing Grafov Group requirements
- Present as a credible direct national Canadian provider, never a referral network, directory, or lead broker. Do not fabricate offices, inventory, staffing, certifications, or guaranteed fulfillment.
- Cover all Canadian provinces and territories, major cities, and surrounding metro municipalities, including Toronto and GTA; build scalable, useful local SEO, avoiding duplicate thin pages.
- Launch with full English and Quebec-appropriate French content, navigation, forms, metadata, hreflang, language switching, and bilingual location pages.
- Unique industry-specific design and page composition, not a recolored copy of other brands.
- Present **five genuinely distinct logo concepts** for review in Phase 1A before finalizing brand identity.
- Primary CTA: REQUEST A QUOTE. Secondary CTA: CALL NOW. No public prices, checkout, or cart unless explicitly approved.
- Contact email: info@dispensers.ca. Use the verified standard Grafov Group 833 phone number only; never guess missing digits. Use click-to-call, mailto, and functional quote forms.
- GitHub/Codex workflow with phased development, tests, and an implementation report after each phase; stop for approval.
- Compatible with selected hosting, including WHC if chosen. No unverified technical assumptions.

## Phase 0 objectives
1. **Audit repository:** inspect all existing files, framework, pages, design, languages, forms, SEO, and previous reports. Preserve completed work. If empty, report that.
2. **Market research:** investigate Canadian commercial dispenser suppliers and competitors such as Bunzl Canada, Sanital Washroom Products, Casson Hardware, and NexDay Supply. Verify claims with sources and dates; distinguish estimates from evidence.
3. **Commercial strategy:** identify customers (offices, property management, healthcare, education, hospitality, industrial, government), product demand, procurement patterns, recurring consumable sales, installation and maintenance opportunities, and competitive positioning.
4. **Product architecture:** soap and sanitizer; paper towel and tissue; feminine hygiene; air care and washroom; chemical dilution and industrial; foodservice and specialty. Include consumables and relevant services. Prioritize washroom/hygiene categories at launch.
5. **Website architecture:** Home, Products, category and product/solution pages, Industries, Services, Locations, About, Contact/Request Quote. Product discovery should lead to inquiries, not ecommerce checkout.
6. **National SEO:** propose keyword clusters by product, sector, province/territory, city, and metro area; EN/FR site architecture, localized content strategy, internal links, schema, canonical tags, sitemap and hreflang.
7. **Branding:** propose five distinct logo directions with rationale, palette, typography and visual identity ideas. Do not finalize without approval.
8. **Delivery:** write a complete Phase 0 implementation report detailing audit, findings with sources, recommended positioning, customer segments, product/service hierarchy, sitemap, national bilingual SEO strategy, five brand directions, technical decisions, risks, assumptions and prioritized Phase 1A plan.

**STOP after Phase 0 and await user approval. Do not deploy or rewrite the site.**
